Data Deletion Request

Quick Paste Keyboard

Last updated: June 2026

This page explains how you can request deletion of your account data and associated information from Quick Paste Keyboard.

Note: Quick Paste Keyboard works fully offline. If you never signed in with Google, all your data is stored only on your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app. No cloud deletion request is needed in that case.

What data is stored when you sign in

If you enabled the optional Cloud Sync feature and signed in with Google, the following data is stored in your Firebase account space:

  • Your Google account email address (used as your account ID)
  • Your snippets and folders (synced for backup and cross-device restore)
  • Your app settings associated with the synced account

How to delete your data

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Quick Paste Keyboard on your device.
  2. Go to Settings &​gt; Cloud Sync.
  3. Tap "Sign out" — this disconnects your Google account from the app.
  4. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored snippets from your device.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

To request full deletion of your cloud-synced data, send an email to the developer:

Email: aliaminchohan456@gmail.com

Subject: Delete my synced data — Quick Paste Keyboard

Please include:
  • The Google email address you used to sign in
  • A short request to delete your account and synced data

Your deletion request will be processed within 30 days. After deletion, your email address and all synced snippets/folders will be permanently removed from our Firebase database and cannot be recovered.

What is deleted vs. kept

  • Deleted: Your email address, synced snippets, synced folders, and synced settings from our Firebase database.
  • Kept (if required by law): Certain logs may be retained briefly for security/fraud-prevention purposes as required by applicable law, then permanently deleted.
